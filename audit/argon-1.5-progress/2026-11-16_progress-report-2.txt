3. Explanation of the Earlier Unaligned 4× Run (976 Ticks & 10,020 ms)
 * Why 1,000 ticks showed 10,020 ms in the earlier ad-hoc table: core/core_x86.js computes SYS_TIME_MS ([0x0FFD24]) as imul eax, [0x0FFF08], 10 ONLY inside sys_02 (hal_yield()), whereas the IRQ0 ISR increments [0x0FFF08] (irq0_ticks) asynchronously on every PIT tick (11,931 cycles at 100 Hz). Sampling [0x0FFD24] and [0x0FFF08] on arbitrary CPU steps between sys_02 calls read [0x0FFF08] 2 ticks (20 ms) ahead of the last sys_02 update. Sampling immediately on 0x0FFD04 (presentCount) transitions after sys_02 guarantees SYS_TIME_MS === irq0_ticks * 10 identically.
 * Why the earlier unaligned 4× run recorded 976 ticks over ΔRTC = 10 s (Measurement Error, NOT Dropped IRQ0s): Hummingbird's CMOS RTC (ports 0x70/0x71, register 0x00) derives seconds from Math.floor(cpu.cyclesExecuted / cpu.cyclesPerSecond). At 4× speed (10 MHz), boot_splash() reached frame 1 (0x0FFD04 == 1) at 2,400,000 cycles = 0.24 s (24 PIT ticks) into the initial RTC second because the measurement window did not wait for a CMOS 0x00 seconds rollover before starting. Waiting for rtcSec - rtcStart === 10 therefore spanned only 9.76 s (97,600,000 cycles = 976 PIT ticks), not 10.00 s. When aligned to a CMOS 0x00 seconds rollover at both start and stop, 1×, 4×, and 16× all produce Δirq0_ticks = 1,000 and ΔSYS_TIME_MS = 10,000 ms for ΔRTC = 10 s with zero dropped IRQ0s.
